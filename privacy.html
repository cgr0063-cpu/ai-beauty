AI Beauty Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Beauty collects, uses, processes, stores and shares information when you use the AI Beauty mobile application.

Information We May Process

Depending on the features you choose to use, AI Beauty may process:

  • Account and authentication information.
  • Photos and camera content, including selfies, Fit Check photos and images you choose to upload for personalized beauty, style and look recommendations.
  • Location information when you grant location permission, for location or weather-aware recommendations.
  • Microphone or video content only when you choose a feature that requires audio or video recording.
  • Preferences and profile information that you voluntarily provide for personalization.
  • Purchase and subscription information required to provide premium features. Payment processing is handled by the applicable app store or payment provider. AI Beauty does not receive your full payment-card details.
  • Technical and usage information needed to operate, secure, diagnose and improve the application.

How We Use Information

Information is used to provide AI Beauty features, generate personalized recommendations, operate accounts and subscriptions, provide requested location-aware functionality, maintain security, troubleshoot problems and improve the application.

AI Processing and Service Providers

Information submitted to AI-powered features may be transmitted to backend infrastructure and service providers acting on our behalf so that the requested feature can be performed.

Service providers may also be used for hosting, authentication, subscription management, application distribution, diagnostics and other technical operations.

Camera, Photos, Location and Microphone

AI Beauty requests device permissions only when they are required for a feature you choose to use.

Camera and photo access may be used for selfies, Fit Check and saved looks. Location access may be used for location or weather-aware recommendations. Microphone access is used only for features requiring audio recording.

You can control these permissions through your device settings.

Sharing of Information

AI Beauty does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers only when necessary to operate requested features, provide subscriptions, comply with legal requirements, or protect users and the service.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information during transmission and processing. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, meet legitimate operational requirements and comply with legal obligations.

Where applicable, users may request deletion of their account and associated personal information.

Your Choices

You may decline optional permissions, change device permissions, choose not to upload optional content and request access to or deletion of personal information where applicable.

Children

AI Beauty is not intended for children under 13. If we learn that personal information from a child under the applicable minimum age has been collected without appropriate authorization, reasonable steps will be taken to delete it.

International Processing

Information may be processed in countries other than your country of residence where our infrastructure or service providers operate.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when our services, practices or legal requirements change. The effective date shown above will be updated when appropriate.
